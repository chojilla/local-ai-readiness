Can your computer run a local AI model?

A 2-minute self-assessment for clinical faculty. Explore whether your personal computer can run Ollama and Qwen3, without sending prompts to a cloud AI provider.

1. Tell us about your computer

2. Install and test

Download Ollama from the official website, then open PowerShell (Windows) or Terminal (Mac) and run:

ollama run qwen3:4b

First run downloads the model. You need internet access for the download, but a locally downloaded model can subsequently run offline. No dedicated GPU is required, although CPU-only responses may be slow.

3. Try a clinical manuscript exercise

Use a public, open-access manuscript. If your interface supports PDF attachments, attach the manuscript; otherwise paste the abstract and selected methods/results text. Then use this prompt:

Using only the manuscript text supplied, summarize (1) study question and design, (2) population, (3) primary endpoint, (4) numerical findings, and (5) three limitations. Distinguish author-reported limitations from your own inferences. Do not invent missing data; say when the supplied text is insufficient.

Verify: Compare all numerical results and conclusions against the original paper. Text-only models may not interpret figures or scanned tables, and retrieval may omit important passages.

Responsible-use reminder

  • Use public manuscripts or approved synthetic educational material only.
  • Do not place identifiable patient information or confidential institutional files on a personal computer without authorization.
  • Local inference is not the same as guaranteed security. Check cloud integrations, storage, backups, and network exposure.
  • These are practical estimates, not guaranteed compatibility or performance benchmarks.
